# Presenting the Barak Salam Platform

A 15-minute walk through one full season for the Chairman, the DG and PVARA. Everything is driven from the **Sandbox** pill in the bottom-right corner: it switches between people and moves the season date.

## Before you start

1. Open the link in Chrome on a laptop and go full screen.
2. Sandbox → **Reset demo data** (click twice). The season starts again with the market open: on today's date until 4 Nov, and on 14 Oct after that.
3. Leave **Run Barak's steps on the way** ticked. Moving the date then runs what Barak's team would do: closing the market, signing growers, recording deliveries, running the sales.
4. Moving the date backwards only changes the date; what already happened stays. Reset to replay.
5. Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, so it works on any network.

## What each audience cares about

- **Chairman:** the business in one season. Show steps 1, 5 and 9: retail demand, the pilot's economics, and money that reconciles.
- **DG:** crop monitoring with LIMS. Show step 5 (the crop card and "Open LIMS crop monitoring") and step 7 (Crop monitor).
- **PVARA:** investor protection and the token. Show step 3 (key facts and consent), step 5 (simulated tokens, clearly not issued) and step 8 (controls, proof of reserve, hash chain).

## Questions you may get

- **Are the tokens live?** No. Until PVARA approves, contracts are physical Salam. The platform shows how each contract would be recorded as PAT (1 PAT = 1 kg), hash-chained like the live ledger. Hedera anchoring follows approval.
- **Where does investors' money sit?** In an escrow (client) account at the bank. Barak cannot spend it. It leaves only to a signed, paid grower or back to the investor.
- **What if the crop falls short?** The grower delivers later (up to 30 days) or refunds the price of the missing wheat, with no profit added. There is no crop insurance this season; takaful is planned from Rabi 2027-28.
- **Is it Shariah-compliant?** It is structured as Salam under AAOIFI Shariah Standard 10, with Barak as agent under Wakalah. The contracts are drafted for review by legal counsel and an independent Shariah advisor.
- **What is real and what is simulated?** The rules, calculations, contract flow, ledger and reconciliation are all real logic. Identity checks, bank payments, SMS, satellite readings, the price path and the 24 other investors are simulated.
- **Where do the prices come from?** The PAT Pricing Engine on 31 Oct 2026: the Rs 4,500 federal indicative price for the 2027 crop, a September median of Rs 4,875 and the SBP rate of 12%.
